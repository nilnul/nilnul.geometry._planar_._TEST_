# Visualise Kakeya Conjecture using Nilnul Framework

Recently a breakthrough on Kakeya problem is achieved by a PKU alumni.

Visualization of the trail of a needle will help us understand the problem better.

`nilnul.geometry._planar_._LIB_` is a lib for us to model 2d shapes as well as the morphs( or transforms as called by most of us) including the the trails of such shapes.

Here we use code to generate a gif to animate such a locus, helping us understand the sweep of a needle inside a certain space, which in turn can be further expanded in the future to demonstrate Kakeya problem.

Just run:
https://github.com/nilnul/nilnul.geometry._planar_._TEST_/blob/nilnul-pub/curve_/roulette_/cog_/hypocycl_/tusi/UnitTest1.cs#L15
,to generate and open the gif. And you can also adjust the proportion of the two circles in order to generate different gifs.
